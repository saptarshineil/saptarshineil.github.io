In preparation SpectralCTGaussians: Projection-domain reconstruction and basis material decomposition for spectral CT using 3D Gaussian splatting R. Vos, S. N. Sinha, M. Weinmann
Spectral CT reconstruction with 3D Gaussian splatting — one shared representation for all energy channels. Material decomposition in a single step, allowing direct basis material segmentation.
In preparation AgenticCADedit: A stateful, tool-mediated agentic approach to multimodal 3D CAD editing S. N. Sinha, M. S. Goschke, P. J. Kühn, A. Kuijper, M. Weinmann
CAD editing as small, verifiable steps on a persistent model state instead of regenerating whole programs. The agent checks its own geometry and reverts single faulty operations — higher success rates at lower token cost.
