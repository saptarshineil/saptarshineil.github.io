Per-Gaussian basis material fractions and a differentiable polychromatic forward model extend 3D Gaussian Splatting to spectral CT, enabling novel view synthesis, spectral volume reconstruction and one-step basis material decomposition from a single shared representation. Paper Project page
Preprint · 2026 AgenticCADedit: A Stateful, Tool-Mediated Agentic Approach to Multimodal 3D CAD Editing S. N. Sinha, M. S. Goschke, P. J. Kühn, A. Kuijper, M. Weinmann
CAD editing becomes a sequence of small, verifiable actions on a persistent CAD state: incremental code steps commit to the session, geometry is inspected and rendered for verification, and faulty operations are reverted individually. Preprint · 2026 (upcoming in IEEE Computer Graphics & Applications) Curating Synthetic Data for Task-Specific Visual Perception S. N. Sinha, P. J. Kühn, M. Weinmann
The central question for specialized vision systems is not how to generate more data, but which data to generate. Curated synthetic data, with scene content, appearance, sensing characteristics, and annotations designed around a task, is examined through three paradigms: procedural rendering, physically-based simulation, and generative AI. Illustrated on industrial defect detection, autochrome plate restoration, and 6DoF pose estimation from RGB and event data, hybrid pipelines that combine controllable supervision with learned appearance emerge as the most promising route to sim-to-real transfer.
