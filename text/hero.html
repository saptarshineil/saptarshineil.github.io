Saptarshi Neil Sinha, Ph.D.

Senior 3D Vision & Perception Scientist

I am a 3D vision and perception scientist with a background in computer graphics. I work on real-time rendering, in particular spatial data structures that enable loading large 3D models in the browser. My current focus is learning-based scene digitization and vision perception beyond RGB cameras. This includes perception tasks trained on synthetic data — object detection on the edge, surface defect detection, and pose estimation — as well as multi-modal CAD editing with large language models and agentic systems. My research interests are real-time rendering, learning-based scene digitization, synthetic data generation, image restoration and multi-modal scene understanding, with applications in the content and creative industries, automotive, smart farming and medical imaging.
